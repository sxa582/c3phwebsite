About this portfolio

NIH records are grouped by core award number and, where applicable, subproject. NIH annual amounts describe the listed award or subproject at its recipient institution. The PCORI amount is the full multisite study budget. These figures do not represent C3PH’s allocation. Earlier award records are identified separately.

This selected portfolio includes research led by C3PH investigators with collaborating institutions. NIH information comes from RePORTER; Pressure Check funding and investigator roles are linked to PCORI and the study team. Updated October 22, 2026.
