Project portfolio / Noise & incident diabetes
Exposome

Transportation noise and metabolic health

A retrospective study examines transportation noise exposure and the development of type 2 diabetes.

The clinical question

The environments in which people live may influence cardiometabolic health.

The approach

The study linked transportation noise exposure with incident diabetes in an observational analysis.

What the study adds

This publication examines the association between transportation noise and type 2 diabetes; the paper provides the estimates and methodological details.

The next question

Which environmental factors may inform prevention strategies at the population level?
