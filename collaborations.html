Starting a collaboration

Tell us what you want to understand.

Share a brief overview so the team can identify the relevant expertise and discuss feasibility.

c3phteam@houstonmethodist.org

Helpful details to include

  • Your research question and intended clinical or population benefit.
  • Your institution, collaborators, and relevant methods or data.
  • The expertise or validation support you are seeking.
  • Your proposed timeline and funding context, if available.

Describe data at a high level. Do not send patient-level or confidential information by email.
